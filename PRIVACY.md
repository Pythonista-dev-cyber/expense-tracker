# Privacy Policy — Pythonista's Expense Tracker

_Last updated: 19 September 2026_

Pythonista's Expense Tracker ("the app") is a desktop app for recording your personal expenses.
This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: **your data never leaves
your computer.**

## What the app stores

The app stores only what you enter:

- expense amounts, categories, dates and notes
- your chosen currency symbol

This is saved in a file on your own computer (in your Windows user profile's app data folder).
It is not sent anywhere.

## What the app does not do

- It does **not** collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information.
- It does **not** use analytics, tracking, advertising or cookies.
- It does **not** require an account or sign-in.
- It does **not** connect to the internet to store or process your data.

## Exporting your data

If you use **Export CSV**, the app writes a file to a location you choose. What you do with that
file is up to you.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes its data (Microsoft Store version). You can also delete individual
expenses inside the app at any time.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
