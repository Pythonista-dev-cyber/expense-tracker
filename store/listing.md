### Description

```
Take control of your spending with a beautiful, fast and private expense tracker.

Pythonista's Expense Tracker turns your everyday spending into clear, glanceable insights. Add an expense in seconds, see where your money goes by category, and compare this month with last month on a smooth, animated chart.

Everything stays on your PC. There's no account to create, no ads, no tracking and no cloud. Your data is yours.

HIGHLIGHTS
• Add an expense in seconds with one-tap categories
• Animated spending chart that compares this month with last month
• Category breakdown with a glowing donut chart and ranked list
• Transactions grouped by day, with instant search

• Export everything to CSV for Excel or Google Sheets

Built with care for Windows 10 and Windows 11.
```
